Privacy Policy

Quran Reel Maker (quranreelmaker.xyz) · last updated 13 October 2026

Quran Reel Maker is a free website that makes short Quran videos (reels). There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What we collect

  • The choices you make (surah, ayat, translation language, background, and any question you type into "Ask the Quran") are sent to our server only to make your reel or answer. They are not linked to you and are not stored after that.
  • Videos you make on our server are deleted automatically within about an hour. Videos made "on this device" never leave your browser.
  • Your IP address is kept in memory for a few minutes only, to stop abuse (speed limits). It is not written to disk or shared.
  • Anonymous daily totals (how many pages were opened and reels made, and which surahs, languages and Ask the Quran topics were used) help the site owner improve the site. They contain no addresses, no text you type and nothing that identifies you.
  • Your browser remembers your last language and background choice (local storage) on your own device. You can clear it at any time.

We do not use cookies for visitors, analytics, advertising or tracking, and we never sell or share data.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare delivers the website and protects it from attacks. It may process your IP address and request details under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Quran text, recitation and translations are loaded from Quran.com, alquran.cloud and islamic.network by our server. Your browser may play recitation audio directly from these sources.

Donations

If a donation box is shown, paying through its QR code or link happens entirely in your own payment app (e.g. UPI). The site never sees or stores any payment details.

YouTube

Our own YouTube channel posts Quran reels made by this project, using the YouTube API Services. This uses only the site owner's YouTube account, only to upload videos to that channel. No visitor ever signs in with Google or YouTube, and we access no visitor's YouTube or Google data. See the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. The owner can remove this access at any time from Google security settings.

Children

The site collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
